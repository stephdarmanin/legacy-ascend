Returns & Refunds Policy

Effective 24 November 2024 · Last updated 24 November 2024
This is the current text and still refers to "Legacy Life Coaching & Consulting." It will be updated to Legacy Ascend. See OUTSTANDING.md → "Rebrand legal copy".

We are committed to providing valuable coaching services and high-quality digital products to customers around the world. This policy applies to all purchases made through our website. Due to the nature of coaching services and digital goods, we have specific policies regarding returns and refunds. Please read carefully.

1. Coaching Sessions, Packages & Programs

All sales of coaching services — individual sessions, packages, and programs — are final upon purchase. Due to the nature of our services, we do not offer refunds once a session has been booked or completed. This extends to promotional offers, discounted sessions, or packages, which are non-refundable and non-transferable.

2. Appointments

If you wish to reschedule, please notify us at least 24 hours in advance. Sessions cancelled with adequate notice may be rescheduled without penalty.

3. How to Request a Reschedule

Email hello@stephdarmanin.com. In the subject line, include your name, "reschedule appointment," and the appointment date and time. State the reason in the email. We respond within 24 hours.

4. Satisfaction Guarantee

While we cannot offer refunds for services, we want you to be satisfied. If a session did not meet your expectations, contact us within 72 hours and we will do our best to address your concerns.

5. Digital Product Purchases

All sales of digital products — downloadable content, e-books, software, and other digital files — are final once the purchase is completed and the product delivered.

6. Change of Mind

Due to instant access, we do not accept returns or offer refunds for change of mind. Please review product descriptions and any previews before purchasing.

7. Faulty or Not as Described

If a digital product is faulty or not as described, contact us within 7 days of purchase with proof of purchase and details of the issue. If we determine the product is faulty or not as described, we will provide a remedy — a replacement or refund at our discretion.

8. How to Request a Refund

Email hello@stephdarmanin.com. In the subject line include your name, "issue with order," and the digital product name. State the reason in the email. We respond within 14 business days.

9. Exceptions

Certain products may be excluded, such as custom or personalized digital products, and products that have been downloaded.

10. International Clients

For clients outside Australia: sessions are conducted in English and subject to our local time zone (Sydney NSW). Contact us with any concerns regarding international transactions.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Changes will be posted with an updated effective date.

No-Show & Cancellation Policy

When you book an appointment, that time is set aside for you. If you do not show up and have not given at least 24 hours' notice, you will be required to pay the full cost of the booked session. In genuine emergencies, the Company at its sole discretion will determine whether a refund can be granted; qualified refunds are processed within 14 business days.
