# Brand & Visual Direction

## The feeling
Premium, luxurious, classic. Geared toward **men and women** (not overtly feminine). The
instant feeling should be **striking, authoritative, professional** — powerful, never
childish, stable (think pillars, marble statues) and elemental (water, lightning,
crystals, sand dunes).

## Colours (already set in `css/style.css`)
- Deep teal: `#1a4a5a` · `#123642` · `#0d2a33`
- Cream / off-white: `#F5F0E8` · `#EFE8DC`
- Gold/brown: `#8B5E3C` · `#a36f45` · `#6d4a2f`
- Light **and** dark backgrounds are both in scope. Occasionally near-black brown for type.

## Fonts
- **Cormorant Garamond** — headings, prices, large numbers.
- **Inter** — body, UI, labels.

## Logo
- White sun/moon mark on the dark toolbar (rendered white via CSS).
- `assets/logos/legacy-ascend-icon.jpg` is the square icon used for favicon + social shares.
