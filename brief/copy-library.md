## About Steph (condensed — the version to use)

Steph Darmanin is an international bestselling author, keynote speaker, performance
coach, and MC whose life is living proof of what becomes possible when you choose
yourself — fully, and without apology.

After navigating burnout, debt, a toxic relationship, and the quiet exhaustion of
performing success rather than living it, Steph did what most people talk about and few
actually do: she invested in herself, did the inner work, and rebuilt from the inside
out. That decision changed everything. It also became her life's work.

Today she draws on a powerful suite of certifications — ICF Accredited Coaching, NLP,
CBT, EFT, Breathwork, Law of Attraction & Abundance, Mindfulness, and Usui Holy Fire
Reiki — alongside two decades of experience across sales, brand strategy, media, and
events. She holds a degree in English Literature from the University of Toronto and has
authored four #1 international bestselling titles. Her mission: to help high achievers
close the gap between who they are and who they know they could be.

Tagline: **"Your legacy starts the moment you choose yourself."**

## FAQs (homepage = short; full list lives on Work With Me)
1. **What is coaching and how does it work?** Collaborative, future-focused process to
   clarify goals, identify obstacles, and build strategies. Not a substitute for medical
   care.
2. **What types of coaching do you offer?** Health & wellness, career development,
   relationships, performance.
3. **How do I know which program is right for me?** Depends on your season of life — book a
   free Discovery Call.
4. **How do I know if coaching is right for me?** Ideal if you're stuck, seeking clarity,
   and want accountability. Not a quick fix.
5. **How long does coaching take to work?** Varies — weeks to months depending on goals and
   how fully you integrate the work.
6. **Do you offer coaching in person?** Sessions are virtual (Google Meet); occasional
   in-person events.
7. **Are sessions confidential?** Yes, strictly.
8. **How do I get started?** Book a Discovery Call.

## Ideal Customer Profiles (summary)

**Private coaching personas:**
- **The Burnt-Out Corporate Professional** (35–52, senior). Looks successful; privately
  depleted, grieving a lost version of themselves. Wants permission to want something
  different. Needs a coach who understands corporate from the inside.
- **The High-Achieving Creative Business Owner** (late 30s–early 50s). Built something
  real; success became the drug; creativity and relationships in the margins. Wants to feel
  like a whole person again.
- (Brief also references **the Everyman / general coaching client** and **the Job Seeker**
  as ICPs for the quiz.)

**Corporate workshop ICP:** SMB, mid-market (sweet spot — Aurora Energy Research sits
here), enterprise, and large enterprise. Buyers: Sales/Commercial leaders (revenue case)
and People & Culture / L&D (retention, engagement, leadership pipeline). The hook:
communication is the last mile of performance, and it's undertrained.

## Concrete outcomes clients receive (proof bank for sales copy)

**After ~8 weeks of private coaching, a client HAS:** a written values framework; a
standards document; a life architecture map; a repeatable daily practice; a personal
leadership identity statement; at least one major decision finally made.

**They STOP:** deciding by other people's expectations; outsourcing self-worth to
productivity; numbing; living in "almost"; shrinking; apologising for having standards.

**They START:** values-led decisions; clear communication of needs and boundaries; showing
up as the main character; recognising self-sabotage in real time; investing in themselves
without guilt.

**Corporate workshop outcomes the buyer cares about:** revenue/new-business conversion,
client retention, leadership pipeline & promotion readiness, engagement scores, brand
reputation, meeting efficiency. One-liner: *"Every time your team presents, they're either
winning or losing trust, business, and opportunity. This workshop makes sure they're
winning."*
