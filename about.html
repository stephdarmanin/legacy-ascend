Steph Darmanin is an international bestselling author, keynote speaker, performance coach, and MC whose life is living proof of what becomes possible when you choose yourself — fully, and without apology.

After navigating burnout, debt, a toxic relationship, and the quiet exhaustion of performing success rather than living it, Steph did what most people talk about and few actually do: she invested in herself, did the inner work, and rebuilt from the inside out. That decision changed everything. It also became her life's work.

Today she draws on a powerful suite of certifications — ICF Accredited Coaching, NLP, CBT, EFT, Breathwork, Law of Attraction & Abundance, Mindfulness, and Usui Holy Fire Reiki — alongside two decades of real-world experience across sales, brand strategy, media, and events. Her mission is simple: to help high achievers close the gap between who they are and who they know they could be, with clarity, purpose, and a life that actually feels like theirs.
