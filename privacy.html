Legacy Life Coaching & Consulting (the "Company") is committed to maintaining robust privacy protections for its users. This Privacy Policy is designed to help you understand how we collect, use, and safeguard the information you provide, and to assist you in making informed decisions when interacting with our website, services, and other digital platforms.

For the purposes of this Policy, "Site" refers to the Company's website. "We," "us," and "our" refer to the Company. "You" refers to you, as a user of our Site or Service. By accessing our Site or Service, you accept this Privacy Policy and our Terms of Use, and you consent to our collection, storage, use, and disclosure of your Personal Information as described here.

1. Information We Collect

We collect "Non-Personal Information" and "Personal Information."

  • Non-Personal Information includes information that cannot personally identify you, such as anonymous usage data, general demographic information, referring/exit pages and URLs, platform types, and submitted preferences.
  • Personal Information includes your name, email address, phone number, and any other information you submit when contacting us, signing up for coaching, or purchasing products.
  • Payment Information: when you make a purchase we may collect payment details. All payment data is processed securely by third-party payment processors; we do not store your payment details.

2. Information Collected via Technology

To improve the quality of the Service, we track information provided by your browser or our software when you use the Service — such as the referring URL, browser type, device, and time and date of access. We track this using cookies (both persistent and session). Sending a cookie to your browser enables us to collect Non-Personal Information and keep a record of your preferences.

3. Children's Privacy

The Site and Service are not directed to anyone under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If we learn that we have gathered personal information from a child under 13 without parental consent, we will delete it as soon as possible. Contact hello@stephdarmanin.com with concerns.

4. How We Use and Share Information

We may use the information we collect to provide coaching services, communicate with you (updates, reminders, newsletters — you can opt out anytime), improve our services, and process payments. Except as stated in this Policy, we do not sell, trade, or rent your Personal Information for marketing purposes without your consent. We may share Personal Information with vendors performing services for us, who use it only at our direction. We may disclose information where reasonably necessary to meet legal process, enforce our terms, address fraud or security concerns, or protect the rights, property, or safety of users, the Company, or the public.

5. How We Protect Information

We implement security measures — including encryption, firewalls, and secure socket layer technology — designed to protect your information from unauthorized access. However, no method is completely secure, and by using our Service you acknowledge and agree to assume these risks.

6. Your Rights

  • Access and Correction: request access to your personal information and corrections where inaccurate.
  • Deletion: request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal retention obligations.
  • Opt-Out: unsubscribe from promotional emails at any time. We may still send administrative emails such as Privacy Policy updates.

7. Links to Other Websites

We may provide links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices. This Policy applies solely to information collected through our Site and Service. We encourage you to read the privacy statements of other websites before interacting with them.

8. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to change this Policy at any time. We will notify you of significant changes by email or a prominent notice on the Site; significant changes take effect 30 days after notification. Please check this page periodically for updates.

9. Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Email hello@stephdarmanin.com.
